# Balla-Gabriele Ferdinand

Afrocaribbean-French, LA-based Researcher, educator, and product builder working at the intersection of accessibility, community-centered research, and technology.

My work focuses on making complex systems easier to understand, navigate, and participate in, particularly for people whose needs are often overlooked in conventional design processes.

## What I work on

**[Ultravocal](https://ultravocal.app)**  
An early-stage legal navigation platform exploring accessible, privacy-conscious tools for people navigating complex legal processes.

My work includes translating accessibility principles and usability findings into product requirements, coordinating AI-assisted implementation, and conducting iterative testing.

**Equipped & Resourced (https://www.equippednresourced.com/)**  
A community-centered education and accountability practice grounded in participatory learning, survivor-centered facilitation, and practical institutional change.

## How I work

- **Research and participation:** Understanding barriers and translating needs into practical decisions.
- **Accessibility and user agency:** Designing experiences that accommodate different preferences without requiring personal disclosure.
- **Product development:** Defining requirements, coordinating implementation, documenting decisions, and testing user journeys.
- **Community accountability:** Creating opportunities for meaningful participation and ensuring that feedback informs decisions.

## Selected work

**Designing Accessibility Around User Choice**

A short case study documenting how accessibility principles informed configurable interface preferences in Ultravocal, including implementation decisions, founder-led testing, and areas requiring further participatory validation.
